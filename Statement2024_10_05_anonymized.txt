Settlement Date This is the date as specified on your trade confirmation or in the Cash Movements Schedule. This relates to the date that a
bargain was settled, not the execution date.
Portfolio Valuation This is a summary of the valuation of your Account.
Stock Valuation This Stock  Valuation gives you detailed information  on your share holdings in your account.  The  information is based on
the close of business prices applicable to the statement period.  The valuation includes all stock held in your account. Any
stocks  you  sold  before  the  statement  period  end  date  are  not  included.   This  does  not  include  information  regarding
accrued interest  on  interest  bearing stocks.   This  can be  obtained by contacting  us directly  on the telephone number  on
the covering letter.
Holding This is the number of shares held in your account.
Item Description The name of the company, type of share or stock and its nominal  value.  The nominal value is the value given to a share
when it is first authorised and issued by a company.  It has no relation to the current market value of a share.
Market Price The closing  price of  investments  on  the valuation  date.  For  shares,  the closing price is  calculated and published  by the
London Stock Exchange  or relevant exchange  and is always  shown in sterling.   For  Unit  Trusts,  the price quoted will  be
the last bid price issued by the Fund Manager that day.  For Open Ended Investment Companies (OEICs), the price will be
the last single price issued by the Fund Manager on that day.
Market Value This is worked out by taking the number of shares held in your account multiplied by the market price.
Cash Movement Schedule If applicable, this summarises the movements of money and stock as a result of a purchase or sale, cash payments in/out
of your  Account and  interest  and fees  paid.  It  includes trade  confirmation information  for  payments for  purchases, sales,
and  also  information  on  corporate  actions  and  dividends  received  on  your  investments.   For  sales  and  purchases  the
information  provided  is  based  on  the  settlement  date.  Debits  (DR)  -  the  amount  shown  is  the  amount  you  have  paid
including  dealing  charges  and  tax.   Credits  (CR)  -  the  amount  you  receive  after  dealing  and  other  charges  have  been
deducted.
Consideration - The amount of money for a deal before commission charges and tax i.e. gross amount
Stamp  Duty  -  A  tax  imposed  on  the  buying  of  shares.  The  tax  appears  on  your  trade  confirmation  when  you  buy
shares. Currently Stamp Duty is charged at a rate of 0.5% on share purchases. Note that Stamp Duty for Irish equities
is charged at 1% and there is no Stamp Duty for buying Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs).
PTM Levy - A charge imposed when you sell  or buy shares with an aggregate value of above £10000. The charge is
£1.00 and the money  raised goes to  the Panel  of  Takeovers and Mergers. The  Panel write and enforce the rules by
which takeovers of LSE - listed companies are conducted.
Commission -  Payment made to a stockbroker  when you buy or sell  shares.  In general, the level of  commission you
pay will either be a flat fee (possibly going up in stages according to the size of the deal) or a percentage based on the
size of the deal.
Bargain  conditions  -  A  term  used  by  the  London  Stock  Exchange  to  denote  that  certain  conditions  were  agreed
between the two participants at the time of trading. If you require further information regarding the bargain conditions
of a particular trade this can be obtained by contacting us directly on the telephone number on the covering letter
Rate of Exchange - The rate used where the transaction involves a conversion of currency to sterling.

Cash Valuation This is the value of cash held in your account.
Overall Valuation This is the value of all the stock and cash held in your account.
Overall Book Profit/Loss This is the overall profit or loss for all your shares as a % of the book cost.
Book Cost The total original cost including charges.
N.B.  For  shares  transferred  into  an  account  this  cost  is  provided  by  the  customer.   For  shares  transferred  from  an
approved employee savings related share options and profit related schemes into an Account, this cost is the total market
value of the shares, on the date of transfer.  This cost may affect the overall Book Cost, Sector Cost and Grand Totals.
